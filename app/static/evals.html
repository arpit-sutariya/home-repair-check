How well does it work?

Three things are measured separately: reading photos, reading descriptions, and catching emergencies. The AI only does the first two; the safety rules are plain code. Every number here comes from python -m eval.run_eval.

Test photos: senthilsk/crack_detection_dataset (CC BY 4.0), 15 per class. Descriptions and safety cases were written by the author before the first run. The test sets are small, so treat differences of a few points as noise.
