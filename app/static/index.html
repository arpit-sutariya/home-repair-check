How it works

The AI answers one question, “which problem is this?”. Everything that has to be right every time is plain, tested code.

1 · Safety rules first

Gas, sparks, CO, water near power: hard-coded rules check your words before any AI runs. All 100 safety test cases are permanent regression tests.

2 · AI identifies the problem

CLIP compares your photo with written visual cues; a text model reads your description. Or Claude, with an API key. It says “not sure” instead of guessing.

3 · Code prices it and finds pros

Urgency, sourced price ranges and the quote check come from a knowledge file. Local pros come from real listings, never generated.

General guidance from a photo and a description, not a professional inspection. Price ranges are national averages from public cost guides; local prices vary. Contractor listings aren't vetted. If anything feels unsafe, call a professional or emergency services.
